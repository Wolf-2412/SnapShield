# 3-minute demo script

1. Open SnapShield dashboard.
2. Show the privacy indicator: telemetry is processed locally.
3. Inject a benign synthetic event.
4. Inject a suspicious port-scan event.
5. Inject a high-risk encoded-PowerShell event.
6. Show risk score, category, evidence and recommended action.
7. Open the model/runtime information panel and show the Snapdragon optimization path.
8. Show the benchmark panel with measured latency and resource use from the target HP Snapdragon PC.
9. Close with: “Sensitive security telemetry stays on the endpoint; AI inference is optimized for the local device.”
